Pixstage is a screenshot mockup maker for your phone. Turn plain app screenshots into polished images for Google Play and App Store listings, social posts, portfolios and presentations, in a minute and without a computer.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick one, two or three screenshots, choose a layout and a background, add a caption and export a finished screenshot mockup. You can also share a screenshot to Pixstage from any app, or open it from your gallery with "Edit in".

LAYOUTS
• One screenshot, two side by side, three in a row or a fan
• Adjustable padding and rounded corners
• Crop, rotate and remove the status and navigation bars

BACKGROUNDS AND CAPTIONS
• Gradients, solid colours and a blurred copy of your screenshot
• Captions in sans, serif, mono, handwritten and display fonts

FORMATS
• Square 1:1 and Portrait 4:5 posts
• Story 9:16
• Landscape 16:9
• Play Store 1080 × 1920 app screenshots
Export to PNG, or JPEG when the PNG would be too large.

PIXSTAGE PRO
• Device frames for a real device mockup: phone, foldable, tablet and laptop, in realistic, minimal and clay styles
• 3D tilt with perspective, plus presets like "On a table" and "Isometric"
• Premium backgrounds: mesh gradients, patterns, graphics and glow
• Background colours picked from your own screenshots
• Film grain
• Exports without the watermark
Try every Pro feature on your own screenshot before you buy. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and is restored after a reinstall.

MADE FOR ANY SCREEN
Phones, foldables (including tabletop mode), tablets and Chromebooks. Drag and drop images, undo and redo with keyboard shortcuts, dark and light themes.

Free exports carry a small "Made with Pixstage" watermark. Everything except the Pro features above is free.

Pixstage is made for app developers and designers who need store screenshots for every release, and for anyone who wants an app screenshot, a device mockup or a product shot to look its best.
